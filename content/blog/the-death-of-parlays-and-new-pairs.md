# The Death Of Parlays And New Pairs

When Will and I started building Lattica, we had a good idea it would be useful to people because it's something we wanted for ourselves. It's why our whitepaper is called *Unlocking Liquidity*. We wanted to unlock the liquidity stuck in our own positions.

But if there's anything we've learned in the last 10 months, it's that the potential here is much larger than we initially thought, and it's continuously growing. We had no idea that WARHORSE would enable leverage along with credit. Then we thought that leverage would be a helpful bonus on top of people's existing trade flow. Now, after a week of testing, we realize that leveraged prediction market trading can be something entirely new.

It's really a mix between the best of memecoins, perps, and prediction markets, and we believe it will quickly come to dominance.

## The Current Landscape

Let us go into the mind of an average guy considering coming onchain. He has a few thousand dollars he wants to quickly multiply. Where does he go?

The first option is memecoins. The upside is obvious. If he hits, he's rich. But let's be real, he's not going to hit. He'll be farmed, rugged, or simply lose it all on coins that never had a chance.

He can try perps. There's no rugs here; it's all skill. All he has to do is dedicate his life to perfecting his edge and execution. Definitely possible, but odds are he's not going to be one of the chosen few able to stare at a BTC chart for 20 hours a day.

That brings us to prediction markets. Like perps, this is a skill-based casino, except it's about stuff that people actually find entertaining. The problem here is that the positions are bounded between 0 and 1, which means the upside is capped, which means it's harder to get rich rich if you don't already have a large stack.

Parlays could be a solution, right? Throw some bucks down on a 5-leg parlay, get rich. Wrong. There's a reason why parlays make up such a large percentage of Kalshi's and sportsbooks' revenues, and it's not because traders win on them. They are really nothing more than lottery tickets.

What our hero needs is a market with high, repeatable upside, a low learning curve, and high safety from rugs. Leveraged prediction markets are that market.
